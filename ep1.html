Episode 1. The Club That Made Rashford — And What American Soccer Parents Can Learn From It

From The Inside Scoop Podcast · Topic: All Inside Scoop Episodes

What does it actually take to develop world-class soccer players? In this episode, I sit down with Remi Dejonge from Fletcher Moss Rangers — the legendary grassroots club in Manchester, England that produced Marcus Rashford, Jesse Lingard, and a string of other elite talents. Manchester United has taken notice, recently signing six of their young players.

Remi pulls back the curtain on what youth soccer looks like in Manchester — the culture, the coaching philosophy, and what makes a grassroots program like Fletcher Moss punch so far above its weight. If you've ever wondered what American youth soccer could learn from the way England develops players at the grassroots level, this conversation will open your eyes.
