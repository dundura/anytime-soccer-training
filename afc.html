Parent Getting Started Guide

Follow these 3 simple steps to get your player training with Anytime Soccer Training

STEP 1 CREATE YOUR ACCOUNT
1 Go to anytime-soccer.com
2 Click "Join for Free" and fill out the registration form
3 Enter your Team Code during signup Check your email from your coach for the code
4 Open the Welcome Email and click "Verify Address" Don't see it? Check your spam or junk folder!
5 Log in with your email and password — you're in! Watch Video
STEP 2 ADD PLAYER PROFILE
1 From your dashboard, click "Add Profile"
2 Enter your player's name, age, and details
3 You can use the same contact email for all player profiles Add up to 4 player profiles per account
4 Click "Save" to complete the profile Watch Video
STEP 3 JOIN YOUR TEAM
1 Click "Login" next to your player's profile
2 Go to "MY TEAMS" in the navigation menu
3 Click "Join Team" and search for your team name
4 Click "Request to Join" — you're done! Your coach will approve the request, and you'll be ready to train Watch Video

Can't find your team? Try searching for part of the team name, or ask your coach for the exact name.

BONUS ALREADY HAVE AN ACCOUNT? APPLY TEAM CODE
1 Sign in to your existing account at anytime-soccer.com
2 Go to "Account Management" from your dashboard
3 Enter your Team Code and click "Apply" That's it — no need to click Save! Watch Video

Note: Team Codes unlock your subscription access. You'll still need to join your team separately (Step 3 above).
